Terms & Conditions

Shared flights

For reservations of one to five guests without trip insurance, send your cancellation request in writing at least 72 hours before the scheduled meeting time. For reservations of six to ten guests, give at least 10 days’ written notice to cancel or change the number of guests.

If trip insurance is offered and purchased for an eligible shared flight, you may receive a flight credit for cancellations made at least eight hours before the scheduled meeting time, with up to one year to rebook at no additional charge. Check the terms shown at booking for eligibility and how this benefit applies to your reservation.

Private flights

Send your cancellation request in writing at least 14 days before your scheduled meeting time.

If Montana Sky cancels your flight

The pilot makes the final decision about whether conditions are safe to fly. If Montana Sky cancels your flight, you may reschedule or request a refund, less any applicable trip insurance, processing, or booking fees identified in your reservation terms.

Requests and flight details

Use the written contact method in your Montana Sky confirmation to request a cancellation or change. Include your reservation number and the names of the guests affected. Review your booking confirmation for the meeting location, arrival time, passenger requirements, and any terms specific to your reservation.
