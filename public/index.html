Questions answered

Everything starts with a little curiosity.

For specific operating restrictions and cancellations, review the final policies displayed in the booking form.

Can I book just one or two seats on a shared flight?

Yes. Shared flights are priced per seat. The flight itself requires at least four confirmed passengers in total and accommodates up to ten. If the overall departure does not reach the minimum, the operator will advise you of options under its published policy.

What does a private flight cost?

Private flights are charged per group: $2,000 for one to five guests, $3,100 for six to eight, or $3,700 for nine to ten. The same fares apply to morning and sunset flights.

When and where do we meet?

Departure timing varies with the season and flying conditions. The final meeting location and arrival instructions should be confirmed in your reservation details before your trip.

What happens if the weather does not cooperate?

The pilot decides whether it is safe to fly. If a trip cannot operate, Montana Sky will communicate the options defined in its approved weather and rescheduling policy. Please review the terms at checkout.

Are there age, weight, or health requirements?

Passenger eligibility and accommodations depend on the balloon and operating conditions. Please check the final booking terms or contact the operator before purchasing if you have questions about age, weight, mobility, pregnancy, or a health concern.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation periods and any applicable credits or refund terms will be provided in Montana Sky’s booking conditions. Please review them before completing your reservation.
