Montana Sky

Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you contact Montana Sky or book a flight, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, and details about your reservation and guests. If you make a payment, the checkout may request billing and payment information. The website and booking services may also receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and page activity.

How we use information

We use information to answer inquiries, manage bookings, process payments and refunds, send confirmations and flight updates, and help prevent fraud or respond to legal obligations.

Who may receive information

We may share relevant contact, booking, billing, or technical information with the providers that help operate the website, process reservations and payments, or deliver customer communications. This can include PonoRez for reservations and the payment provider used at checkout. We may also disclose information as required by law or to investigate fraud or address a safety issue.

Cookies and similar technologies

The website and booking services may use cookies or similar technologies to keep pages and checkout working and remember preferences. Your browser lets you manage cookies. Blocking some cookies may affect booking features.

Retention, security and children’s privacy

We keep personal information as needed for the purposes above, including recordkeeping and resolving disputes. We take steps to protect it. An adult should make reservations for children and provide any guest information required for the flight. The website is intended for adults making or managing reservations.

Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. To submit a request or appeal a decision about a request, use the Montana Sky contact information and include “Privacy request” or “Privacy appeal” in your message. We may need to verify your identity.

Policy updates

We post updates to this policy on this page.
